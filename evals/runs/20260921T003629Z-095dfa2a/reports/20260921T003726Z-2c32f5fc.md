## policy-scope

Which NYU student levels and organizations are covered by the conduct policy, and which schools and locations does it cover?

### Expected answer

1. The policy covers undergraduate and graduate students and Student Organizations at NYU.
2. It applies across all NYU schools and locations, including Study Away Sites and Portal Campuses.

### Actual answer

The NYU University Student Conduct Policy covers the following student levels and organizations:

- All undergraduate and graduate students at NYU.
- Student Organizations at NYU, including both Center for Student Life All-University clubs and school-based clubs.
- Individuals within student organizations may be held accountable for violations while acting in official or unofficial capacities.

However, the policy explicitly excludes certain student populations, who are governed by separate handbooks or policies:

- Students enrolled at NYU Abu Dhabi
- Students enrolled at NYU Shanghai
- Students in the Grossman School of Medicine (covered by the NYU Grossman School of Medicine Student Handbook)
- Students in the Long Island School of Medicine (covered by the Long Island School of Medicine Student Handbook)
- Students whose primary enrollment is through University Program's Summer Programs (covered by the High School Programs Handbook)

Regarding the locations and schools to which the policy applies, the policy states it is applicable to all students and Student Organizations at NYU, in all schools and locations, including Study Away Sites and Portal Campuses. The academic standards and non-academic misconduct rules apply broadly to NYU’s undergraduate and graduate students across the university's multiple sites, excluding the above-noted exceptions [1][2][4].

In summary:

**Covered:**
- Undergraduate and graduate students at NYU (excluding those specified below)
- Student organizations (all-University clubs and school-based clubs)
- All schools and locations, including Study Away Sites and Portal Campuses

**Not covered (exceptions):**
- Students at NYU Abu Dhabi
- Students at NYU Shanghai
- Students at Grossman School of Medicine
- Students at Long Island School of Medicine
- Students enrolled primarily in University Program's Summer Programs

This distinction ensures that separate populations are governed under their specific handbooks or policies rather than the general NYU Student Conduct Policy [1][2][4].

- Completion of a practical training course (DS-GA 1009) involving an internship.
