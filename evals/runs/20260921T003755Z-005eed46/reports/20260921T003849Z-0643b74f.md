Which NYU student levels and organizations are covered by the conduct policy, and which schools and locations does it cover?

### Actual answer

**Part 1: Which NYU student levels and organizations are covered by the conduct policy?**

The NYU University Student Conduct Policy applies to:
- **All undergraduate and graduate students** at NYU, across all schools and locations, including Study Away Sites and Portal Campuses [3].
- **Visiting or non-matriculated students** registered and attending a course at NYU, who must abide by the standards for the duration of their enrollment [4].
- **Incoming students** are considered covered once they move into a residence hall or on the first day of their first semester [4].
- **Student Organizations**, which include groups registered with the Center for Student Life, Student Government Assembly, Greek-letter organizations, athletic teams (varsity, club, and intramural), and those recognized by other academic or administrative units [4].

There are explicit exceptions:
- **Students enrolled at NYU Abu Dhabi**
- **Students enrolled at NYU Shanghai**
- **Students in the Grossman School of Medicine**
- **Students in the Long Island School of Medicine**
- **Students primarily enrolled through University Programs’ Summer High School Programs**

These populations have separate or supplemental handbooks or policies [1][2].

**Part 2: Which schools and locations does it cover?**

The policy explicitly covers:
- **All NYU schools, colleges, and institutes**, including the Institute of Fine Arts (IFA), Institute for the Study of the Ancient World (ISAW), Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, and Center for Urban Science and Progress (CUSP) [1][4].
- **All NYU locations**, including:
  - The main New York campus,
  - **Study Away Sites:** NYU Accra, Berlin, Buenos Aires, Florence, London, Madrid, Paris, Prague, Sydney, Tel Aviv, Tulsa, Washington D.C., Los Angeles,
  - **Portal Campuses:** NYU Abu Dhabi and NYU Shanghai—but note, these have exceptions and may enact additional or conflicting policies, which take precedence at those sites [4].

**Summary:**
- The policy broadly covers all student levels (undergraduate, graduate, visiting/non-matriculated, incoming students) and all student organizations, with specified exclusions.
- It applies across all NYU academic units and global locations, with additional or overriding policies at Portal Campuses or Study Away Sites as required by local law [3][4].
