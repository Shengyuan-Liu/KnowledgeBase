## accept-independent-passfail

According to the saved general FAQ, how many DS-GA 1010 credits can count toward the MSDS, and which courses can be counted pass/fail? Include the exclusion and the Industry Concentration allowance.

### Unedited answer

1. The maximum number of independent study (DS-GA 1010) credits that can count toward the MSDS degree is 6 credits [1].

2. Only 1 elective (this does not include the “One Data Science Elective” requirement) and DS-GA 1009 can be taken as pass/fail and count towards the MSDS degree [1].

3. Students in the Industry Concentration can use their 1 elective pass/fail allowance for an Industry Concentration elective [1].

4. Required courses for the degree cannot be taken as pass/fail [1].
