Student Resources Overview

On this page: Financial Aid & Fellowships • Student Career & Professional Development • Student Groups

CDS provides extensive support to help students succeed academically and professionally. Resources include financial aid, career services, and student-led community groups.

Financial Aid & Fellowships (MS)

Details can be found below:

CDS awards tuition scholarships to incoming MS students on a competitive basis.
All applicants are automatically  considered for these awards, which provide partial tuition coverage for up to two years.

Student Career & Professional Development

CDS collaborates with the NYU Wasserman Center for Career Development to support students through the internship and job search process in a number of ways:

The Center hosts company information sessions each fall and spring, where representatives share their data science initiatives and typical project work.
Career fairs connect students directly with recruiters from companies hiring data scientists.
CDS organizes datathons and hackathons, where students work with company datasets and have their solutions judged by industry professionals.
Professional development workshops throughout the year cover resume reviews, interview preparation, and communication skills.

Student Groups

The Center for Data Science fosters a vibrant community through various student-led organizations, including:

CDS Graduate Student Community-Building Group
Data Science Club
Women in Data Science (WiDS)

These groups provide opportunities for networking, professional development, and community building among data science students.
